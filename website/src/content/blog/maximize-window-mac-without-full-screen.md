To maximise a window on a Mac without going full screen, press **Fn-Control-F** or hover over the green button and choose the **Fill** icon under Fill & Arrange. The window grows to fill the desktop, and the menu bar and Dock stay where they are. Both need macOS Sequoia 15 or later; on older versions, a window manager does the same job.

Clicking the green button itself does something different, which is why so many people end up in full screen by accident. Here is how to get a proper maximise, make it the default, and undo it.

## What is the difference between full screen and maximise on a Mac?

| | Full screen | Fill (maximise) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How | Click the green button, or <kbd>Control</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>F</kbd> | <kbd>Fn</kbd>-<kbd>Control</kbd>-<kbd>F</kbd>, or green button → Fill |
| Menu bar and Dock | Hidden until you move the pointer to them | Always visible |
| Where the window lives | Its own desktop space | Your normal desktop |
| Other windows | Not visible alongside | Still there behind it |
| Switch away | Swipe or Control-Left/Right to change space | Click any other window or app |

Full screen suits focused work like watching a video or writing on a small laptop. Fill is what most people coming from Windows mean by "maximise": a big window that is still part of the normal desktop.

## How do I fill the screen without full screen?

Pick whichever is handiest:

1. **Keyboard:** press <kbd>Fn</kbd>-<kbd>Control</kbd>-<kbd>F</kbd>. On keyboards with a globe key, that is the Fn key.
2. **Green button:** hover over the green button in the window's top-left corner (do not click), then click the fill icon under **Fill & Arrange**.
3. **Menu bar:** choose **Window → Fill**.
4. **Drag:** drag the window by its title bar up to the menu bar and let go when the highlight fills the screen.

If dragging to the menu bar opens Mission Control instead, turn off **Drag windows to top of screen to enter Mission Control** in **System Settings → Desktop & Dock**.

## How do I make a double-click on the title bar maximise?

macOS lets you choose what a double-click on a window's title bar does.

1. Open **System Settings → Desktop & Dock**.
2. Find the setting for **double-clicking a window's title bar**.
3. Choose **Fill**. The other choices are Zoom, Minimise and No Action.

Now a double-click on any standard title bar fills the desktop. Option-click the green button when you want the old size back.

## How do I get a window back to its previous size?

- **Option-click the green button.** Apple notes that this returns the window to its previous size after a Fill.
- **Press <kbd>Fn</kbd>-<kbd>Control</kbd>-<kbd>R</kbd>** (Return to Previous Size), which reverses the last tiling command.

## Can I maximise but keep a little space around the window?

Two options:

- **Tiled windows have margins.** Turn this on in the Windows section of **Desktop & Dock** settings and macOS leaves a gap around filled and tiled windows.
- **Almost maximise with a window manager.** In [Windowmac](/), hold **Option** and press <kbd>Return</kbd> to maximise, and press <kbd>Return</kbd> again for **Almost Maximise**, which leaves a margin on every side so you can still see the desktop and click past the window.

The almost-maximise layout is handy on large monitors where a full-size browser is too wide to read comfortably. To see exactly how big a filled window will be on your display, with or without a Dock and gaps, use the [window size calculator](/tools/window-size-calculator/).

## How do I maximise a window on macOS Ventura or Sonoma?

The Fill command and Fn-Control-F came with window tiling in macOS Sequoia 15. On macOS Ventura 13 or Sonoma 14:

- **Option-click the green button.** Apple's getting-started guide for these versions says holding Option while clicking the green button maximises the window instead of making it full screen.
- **Drag the corners** of the window to the screen edges by hand.
- **Use a window manager.** Windowmac works on macOS 13 or later; flick up while holding Option, or press Option-Return.

## Which way should I use?

| You want | Use |
| --- | --- |
| One keyboard shortcut, built in | <kbd>Fn</kbd>-<kbd>Control</kbd>-<kbd>F</kbd> |
| Mouse only | Double-click the title bar, set to Fill |
| A border around the window | Tiled windows have margins, or Almost Maximise |
| Maximise on macOS 13 or 14 | A window manager |
| No menu bar or Dock at all | Full screen |
