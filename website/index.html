How it works

One gesture. No targets to aim at.

  1. ⌥
    01

    Hold

    Hold Left ⌥ Option. A ring appears right under your cursor, so your eyes never leave your work.

  2. 02

    Flick

    Nudge the mouse toward where the window belongs. An outline shows exactly where it will land. Click to step through sizes: half, third, two thirds.

  3. 03

    Release

    Let go and the window snaps into place. Esc or right-click cancels.

New in 1.2 Strip

Lots of windows? Line them up on a strip.

Hold ⌥ and press S. Every window on that screen becomes a column on one strip that scrolls sideways, wider than the screen if it needs to be. Move between columns with the keyboard or a swipe, and a bar at the bottom shows where you are. New windows join next to the one you're in.

Layouts

Every layout, one flick or keypress away.

Put any of these on any direction of the ring or on any key, or make your own.

  • ScreensSend a window to the next display. It keeps its layout and the cursor comes along.
  • StripLine up every window on a screen as scrolling columns. Try it above.
  • StashTuck a window behind the screen edge. Push the cursor against the edge to slide it back.
  • Resize and nudgeLarger, smaller, grow or shrink any edge, move in any direction.
  • UndoStep back through a window's last positions.
  • Links for everythingEvery action has a windowmac:// link for scripts, Shortcuts and Raycast.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

The one-liner is the quickest. The download takes a few extra clicks the first time, and each one is shown below.

Terminal

Quickest: one line in Terminal

Paste this into Terminal and press Return. It puts the latest Windowmac in Applications and opens it, with no security popup.

$ curl -fsSL https://windowmac.vercel.app/install.sh | bash

Want to check it first? Read the script, it's about 100 lines.

Or download the app

Download Windowmac.zip, unzip it and drag Windowmac into Applications. Then follow these steps the first time you open it.

  1. “Windowmac.app” Not Opened Apple could not verify “Windowmac.app”… Move to Trash Done

    Click Done

    macOS shows this the first time. It's normal for free apps outside the App Store. Click Done, not Move to Trash.

  2. Privacy & Security
    “Windowmac.app” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway

    Open Anyway

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom and click Open Anyway. On macOS 13 or 14 you can right-click Windowmac and choose Open instead.

  3. Open “Windowmac.app”? Open Anyway

    Confirm

    Use your password or Touch ID, then click Open Anyway again. You only do this once.

  4. Accessibility
    Windowmac.app

    Allow Accessibility

    When Windowmac asks, turn it on in Privacy & Security → Accessibility. No restart needed.

  5. ⌥

    Hold ⌥ and flick

    Click any window, hold the Option key left of the spacebar, move the mouse where the window should go, and let go.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Open an issue on GitHub.

Is Windowmac really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT license, with no account, no subscription and no tracking. Windowmac never connects to the internet.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

macOS only lets apps move other apps' windows through Accessibility, so every window manager needs it. Windowmac uses it for that and to notice your Option key, nothing else.

Will holding Option get in the way of typing?

Keys that aren't Windowmac shortcuts type as normal, so ⌥ + E or ⌥ + U still make accents. While Option is held, Windowmac's own keys (arrows, C, Z and a few more) move windows instead, so ⌥ + ← won't jump a word. If you use that a lot, switch the trigger to Right ⌥ or the 🌐 key in Settings → Trigger.

Can I change how it looks or which key triggers it?

Yes. Settings lets you pick the trigger key, the ring's size, shape and colour (it follows your macOS accent colour by default), the gap between windows, animation speed, and apps Windowmac should ignore. You can also add your own layouts.

Can I control it from scripts, Shortcuts or Raycast?

Every action has a link, for example open -g "windowmac://action/right-half" or windowmac://screen/next. Run /Applications/Windowmac.app/Contents/MacOS/Windowmac --list to see them all.

How do I update or uninstall it?

To update, run the one-line installer again or replace the app with a new download. macOS asks for Accessibility again after each update, so just turn Windowmac back on. If it ever stops responding, choose Fix Accessibility Permission… from Windowmac's menu bar icon. To uninstall, quit Windowmac from its menu bar icon and move it to the Trash.
